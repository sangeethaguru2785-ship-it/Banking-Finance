Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Stackly

From a single branch in New York to a global digital bank — here's how we've grown, one milestone at a time.

2001

Stackly Is Founded

A single branch opens on Wall Street with a bold idea — banking should serve people, not the other way around.

2006

Digital Banking Launches

We introduce one of the industry's first online banking platforms, bringing the branch to your home.

2013

One Million Customers

A decade of trust pays off as Stackly crosses the 1 million customer milestone across North America.

2018

Global Expansion

We expand operations into 50+ countries, enabling seamless transactions across borders and currencies.

2021

AI-Powered Insights

Our smart analytics platform goes live, giving customers personalised insights to grow their wealth.

2026

A Global Community

Today, 4.5M+ customers across 120+ countries bank with Stackly — and we're just getting started.
